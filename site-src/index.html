Qtopia

A city utility's fiber network has to stay secret today and after large quantum computers arrive. We use quantum keys (BB84) where they pay off, post-quantum cryptography everywhere else, and physics to catch anyone tapping a fiber.

Grid Twin

A 3D model of a quantum-secured utility network on real Chattanooga and Tennessee grids. Tap a fiber with an eavesdropper and watch its error rate climb, the alarm fire, and the network switch keys and reroute on its own.

On this site the fibers run on the in-browser BB84 engine. The “run as real quantum code” buttons need the team's local server and are hidden here.
