Entanglement on this link: usable

Teleportation needs an entangled pair plus a classical message of two bits per qubit, so it is never faster than the fiber. A chain of fibers is usable for entanglement if its end-to-end error rate is under 11%, with errors composing as (1 − 2Qend) = ∏(1 − 2Qi). Swapping nodes never hold the key.
